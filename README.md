# GenAI Onboarding Workshop Kit

A ready-to-run Tier 1 (Foundations) training session for introducing generative AI to non-technical staff — the slide deck, the facilitator run-sheet, and the hands-on worksheet, all in one place.

This is the operational companion to the [AI Enablement Playbook](https://github.com/DinaElSawah/ai-enablement-playbook): the Playbook is the strategy layer (needs assessment, tiered curriculum design), and this kit is one tier of that curriculum, fully built and ready to deliver.

## What's inside

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`GenAI-Foundations-Workshop.pptx`](./GenAI-Foundations-Workshop.pptx) | The 13-slide deck, with timing and delivery cues in each slide's speaker notes |
| [`facilitator-notes.md`](./facilitator-notes.md) | A slide-by-slide run sheet: timing, what to prepare beforehand, and common facilitation pitfalls |
| [`hands-on-exercise.md`](./hands-on-exercise.md) | The worksheet participants fill in during the session's practice segment, plus two worked examples |

## What the session covers

A ~75–90 minute Foundations session: what generative AI actually is (in plain language), a simple five-step prompting framework (Task → Context → Format → Evaluate → Iterate), hands-on practice with a real task, responsible and safe use, and the honest answers to the questions every audience asks ("will this replace my job?").

## How to use this

1. **Adapt before you deliver.** Slide 3's example task and Slide 9's policy reference are placeholders — swap them for something genuinely relevant to your audience and your organization's real policy.
2. **Read the facilitator notes first**, not just the slide notes — they cover setup and the pitfalls that aren't obvious from the deck alone.
3. **Print or share the worksheet** before the practice segment; participants should be filling it in with a real task from their own work, not a hypothetical one.
